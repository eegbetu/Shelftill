Delete your Shelf & Till account

Shelf & Till is provided by CJ Limited. This page explains how to delete your account and what happens to your data.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Shelf & Till and sign in.
  2. Tap your role badge at the top right (it says Owner or Staff).
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Type your password and tap Delete my account for good.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email stillunsolvedw1x@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Straight away: your login (name, email and password) and your list of shops.
  • If you own a shop: the shop closes at once and your staff lose access. All its records (products, prices, stock, invoices, sales, staff list and debtor records) are deleted within 30 days.
  • If you're staff: you're removed from the shop. Sales you recorded stay in the owner's records, because they belong to the shop.

What we keep

Nothing, apart from anything the law requires us to keep. Deleted data can't be recovered.

See also our privacy policy.
