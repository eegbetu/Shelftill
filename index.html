Test Shelf & Till

Thank you for helping! Shelf & Till is an app for shops to record sales, track stock and keep a debtors book. Follow the three steps in order. It takes about 2 minutes.

  • Android phone
  • Same Gmail as your Play Store
  • Keep it 14 days

  1. Join the testers group

    Tap the button, sign in if asked, then tap Join group.

    Open the group

  2. Become a tester

    Come back here after joining, then tap Become a tester on the page that opens.

    Become a tester

  3. Install the app

    Install it from Play Store. Then create a test shop, add a product and try a sale. Please open it every few days.

    Install from Play Store

Seeing “App not available”? Do step 1 first, then wait a few minutes and try step 2 again. Use the same Gmail for every step.

Landed on “My groups” with nothing in it? Tap the 🔍 search icon on that page and search shelf-and-till-tester, then join it.
