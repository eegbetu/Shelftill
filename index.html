Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can sell, add stock and record debts with no network. Everything saves on the phone and uploads by itself when the connection comes back. Owners can set how long staff may sell offline.

Is my shop's data safe?

Each shop's records are locked to that shop. Only the owner and the staff they invite can see them, and the server checks every request. You can also save your own backup file at any time.

Can my staff use it on their own phones?

Yes. Create an invite code in Settings, and each staff member joins with their own account. You choose what each person can do: sell, give credit, add stock, and so on. You can pause anyone at any time.

Does it work on iPhone and computers?

Yes. On iPhone and computers, Shelf & Till runs in the browser. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and it opens like an app. On Android, get it from Google Play.

Can I print receipts?

Yes, on Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (58 mm and 80 mm) through the free RawBT app on Android. You can also share receipts as a picture on WhatsApp.
