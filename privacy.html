Shelf & Till Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Shelf & Till is provided by CJ Limited (“we”, “us”).

In short: Shelf & Till keeps the records a shop enters, such as products, sales, staff and customers who owe money, so the shop can run its till. We don't sell anyone's data, we don't show ads, and each shop's records are kept private to that shop.

1. Who this policy covers

Shelf & Till is a point-of-sale, stock and debtor-records app for small shops. This policy covers shop owners and staff who create an account, and the shop customers whose details a shop chooses to record (for example, a customer buying on credit).

Each shop decides what it records about its own customers. For those records, the shop is responsible for its customers' information, and we store it on the shop's behalf.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Shop details: shop name, address, phone number and receipt message.
  • Shop records: products, prices, costs, barcodes, stock levels, invoices, sales and receipts, and who made each sale.
  • Staff details: names of staff a shop invites, their role and permissions, and when they last opened the app.
  • Customer (debtor) records a shop enters: a customer's name, phone number (optional), notes, what they bought on credit and payments made.

We don't collect your location, contacts or photos. We don't use advertising or tracking tools.

3. Camera and printing

Barcode scanning uses Google's barcode scanner on your phone. The camera image is processed on the phone to read the barcode number. Only the number is used, and no picture is saved or sent to us.

Receipt printing sends the receipt to the RawBT app on your phone, which passes it to your Bluetooth printer. RawBT is a separate app with its own privacy policy.

4. How we use the information

  • To let you sign in and use your shop's till, stock, debtors and reports.
  • To let shop owners add staff and control what each staff member can do.
  • To show sales, stock and debtor records to the people in that shop who are allowed to see them.
  • To reply when you contact us for help.

We don't use shop records for advertising and we don't sell them.

5. Where the information is stored, and who can see it

Information is stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which runs the app's accounts and database. Data is encrypted while it travels between your phone and Google's servers.

Each shop's records can only be read by that shop's owner and the staff the owner has added. Staff only see what their role allows: for example, staff cannot see the owner's reports or other staff members' sales. Other shops using Shelf & Till cannot see your shop's data.

We share information only:

  • with Google, which provides the servers and sign-in service;
  • if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep a shop's records for as long as the shop uses Shelf & Till. If a shop owner asks us to close the shop's account, we delete the shop's records within 30 days, apart from anything we must keep by law.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask us to:

  • see the information we hold about you;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it.

To ask, email us at the address below. If you're a shop's customer, you can also ask the shop directly, because the shop controls its own customer records.

Deleting an account: in the app, tap your role badge at the top right, then Delete my account, and type your password to confirm. Your login is deleted at once. If you own a shop, the shop closes straight away and its records are deleted within 30 days. You can also email us from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. Staff can also ask their shop owner to remove them.

8. Children

Shelf & Till is a business tool for adults. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll tell shop owners in the app.
